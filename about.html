My Story

Ever since I was young, I was constantly captivated by how physical objects work under the hood. Whether taking apart old household gadgets, watching high-speed automotive engine breakdowns, or marveling at towering industrial machinery, I wanted to understand the forces and kinematics turning raw motion into purposeful work.

Choosing Mechanical Engineering for my B.Tech was a natural progression of that curiosity. Currently in my first year, I am building strong fundamentals in physics, engineering mathematics, engineering drawing, and workshop practice.

My ambition is to master computer-aided design (CAD), finite element analysis (FEA), and smart automated mechanical systems. I believe the future of mechanical engineering lies at the intersection of robust structural design and intelligent digital control.
